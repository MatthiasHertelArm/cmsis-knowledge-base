The following semihosting operations are available:

- SYS_CLOCK (0x10)
- SYS_CLOSE (0x02)
- SYS_ELAPSED (0x30)
- SYS_ERRNO (0x13)
- SYS_EXIT (0x18)
- SYS_EXIT_EXTENDED (0x20)
- SYS_FLEN (0x0c)
- SYS_GET_CMDLINE (0x15)
- SYS_HEAPINFO (0x16)
- SYS_ISERROR (0x08)
- SYS_ISTTY (0x09)
- SYS_OPEN (0x01)
- SYS_READ (0x06)
- SYS_READC (0x07)
- SYS_REMOVE (0x0e)
- SYS_RENAME (0x0f)
- SYS_SEEK (0x0a)
- SYS_SYSTEM (0x12)
- SYS_TICKFREQ (0x31)
- SYS_TIME (0x11)
- SYS_TMPNAM (0x0d)
- SYS_WRITE (0x05)
- SYS_WRITEC (0x03)
- SYS_WRITE0 (0x04)
