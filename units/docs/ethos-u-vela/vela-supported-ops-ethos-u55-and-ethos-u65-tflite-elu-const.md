_Part of: Ethos-U55 and Ethos-U65 Specific Operator constraints_

This is a list of constraints that the ELU operator must satisfy in order to be scheduled on the NPU.

- 32-bit feature-maps are not supported.
- IFM and OFM datatypes must match, and must be Int8
- IFM and OFM shapes must match
